Save the four Lab 9 images into this directory before running lab9_bucket.py.

The lab sheet pairs each image with exactly one analysis, and asks for only
that analysis on that image:

  urban.jpg   an urban setting             label recognition
  beach.jpg   a person on a beach          image moderation
  faces.jpg   people showing their faces   facial analysis
  text.jpg    an image containing text     text extraction

The lab sheet links a source for each one. Two of the four figures on the sheet
are PNGs even though the sheet names the objects .jpg, so a .jpg, .jpeg or .png
file with the right stem is accepted here - the object is stored in S3 under the
name above either way, and Rekognition reads the format from the bytes rather
than from the key.

What Rekognition will reject:
  - anything that is not JPEG or PNG (a WebP or HEIC renamed .jpg fails)
  - anything under 80 pixels on either side
  - anything over 15 MB

Check all four without uploading:

  python3 lab9_bucket.py --check

This file is only a placeholder and can be deleted once the images are here.
